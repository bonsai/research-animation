# Papers and Sources — The Skeleton Dance Motion Semantics

Research target: The Skeleton Dance (1929), motion extraction, music-animation relations, and dance pose analysis.

## A. Directly relevant to The Skeleton Dance

### 1. Angı, Çiğdem Eda (2021)
**“Examining the relation between form information and movement in music education: The skeleton dance.”** *Cypriot Journal of Educational Sciences*, 16(2), 641–658.

- DOI: https://doi.org/10.18844/cjes.v16i2.5641
- Article: https://un-pub.eu/ojs/index.php/cjes/article/view/5641
- Relevance: directly discusses the relation between musical form and movement in The Skeleton Dance.
- How to use: derive a time-aligned annotation plan for musical phrases, beats, and movement changes.
- Verification note: read the full text before extracting specific findings or quotations.

### 2. Benner, Julia (2022)
**“Music in Their Bones: Play, Music and Materiality in Disney’s Dancing Skeleton Films.”** In *On Disney*, pp. 155–170.

- DOI: https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-662-64625-0_10
- ResearchGate record: https://www.researchgate.net/publication/362560459_Music_in_Their_Bones_Play_Music_and_Materiality_in_Disney%27s_Dancing_Skeleton_Films
- Relevance: interpretation of music, materiality, play, and the dancing skeleton body.
- How to use: develop semantic annotations for body-percussion and movements where the body functions as a sound-producing object.
- Verification note: confirm chapter text and bibliographic details from the publisher or library copy.
